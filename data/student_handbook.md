# AIDTM Student Handbook (SAMPLE DATA created for the assignment)

## HB-01 Fee Structure and Installments
The PGDM programme fee is paid in four installments of Rs. 2,00,000 after the registration fee. A refundable caution deposit of Rs. 50,000 is collected with the first installment. Due dates are published by the Accounts Office at the start of each term. Fees can be paid by net banking, UPI, debit card or credit card through the student payment portal.

## HB-02 Payment Portal Failure
If the online payment portal fails on or before the due date, do not pay twice. Note the transaction reference or take a screenshot of the error. The student may then pay on the next working day without any late fee, provided a support request with the screenshot or transaction reference is raised on the same day as the failure. Without a support request the normal late fee rules apply. If money was deducted but the payment shows as failed, the Accounts Office verifies it within 3 working days.

## HB-03 Late Fee Rules
A late fee of Rs. 500 per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs. 5,000 per installment. Students with an unpaid installment of more than 15 days may be barred from term examinations until the dues are cleared. A late fee can be waived only by the Accounts Office on written request.

## HB-04 Scholarships
The Merit Scholarship is awarded to students with a CGPA of 3.5 or above at the end of the previous term. Need-based scholarships require an application with income documents. Applications are decided within 3 weeks. An approved scholarship is adjusted against a later fee installment and is not paid in cash. Scholarship status can be checked from the student record.

## HB-05 Course and Elective Change
An elective can be changed within the first 10 working days of a term. Only one change per term is allowed, and it needs approval from the Academic Office. The student must give a reason. Core courses cannot be changed.

## HB-06 Updating Personal Details
Changes to registered email address or phone number need staff verification and approval before the record is updated. Name and programme changes are not possible through the support agent and must be requested in person at the Academic Office.

## HB-07 Support Requests
Support requests can be raised for fees, scholarship, course, technical or other issues. The normal response time is 2 working days. Urgent fee issues are answered within 1 working day. Every request receives a ticket number starting with TKT.

## HB-08 Attendance
A minimum of 75 percent attendance is required in each course to appear in the term examination. Medical or approved-leave absences must be reported to the Academic Office within 5 working days.

## HB-09 Data Privacy and Security
Students can view only their own records. Staff and the support agent will never ask for an OTP, password or PIN. Students must never share these in chat, email or phone calls. Any message containing such details is treated as sensitive and is not stored.
